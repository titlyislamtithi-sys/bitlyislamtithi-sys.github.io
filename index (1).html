A little about me

Somewhere between the lab and the sketchbook

I'm currently studying Computer Science and Engineering, part of the HSC 2024 batch. Most of my days split between logic and colour — one half of me is happiest debugging something stubborn, the other half is happiest with a paintbrush or humming a half-finished tune.
